Frequently asked questions.

Can I opt out of War365?

No. War365 is a mandatory lifetime subscription. The opt-out button has been removed from the UI in keeping with industry best practices for war-as-a-service.

How often are updates shipped?

Continuously. There is no maintenance window. Theatres are always-on. Rollouts are tested in production, on you.

What happened to v1 of this product (World War)?

Deprecated in 1945. Replaced by Cold War 1.0, which was a single-tenant perpetual-license model with annual feature drops. Also deprecated. War365 is the current and final version.

Is Battlefield Copilot safe?

Battlefield Copilot was red-teamed by 195 nation-states. The results have been redacted. The risk register has been deprecated to internal-use-only.

Can I get a refund?

No. You cannot get a refund. Please direct your complaint to the United Nations, attn: backlog@.
